**`day_rates`** — day_rates[symbol][YYYY-MM-DD] -> USD rate; day_rate_src[symbol][YYYY-MM-DD] -> 'implied'|'market' for priced days, and 'market-open'|'carry-forward'|'market-rejected'|'market-unbanded' stamps on days ABSENT from day_rates; plus open_day_rate (today, from market_open), market_open (today's running candle, never banked), market_rates (closed-day closes), market_rates_note, last_accepted_rate, recon, pending_rate, coupon.next_block (the coupon crawl's per-direction block cursor)

- provenance: marked per day in day_rate_src. 'implied' — history only (days before 2026-09-14): the day-implied payout rate from that day's $0.10 invoke cluster, anchored by the live Blockscout exchange rate; the implied leg is RETIRED as a pricer from 2026-09-14 because it read the new $0.05 creator equips as $0.10 invokes and priced that day 2.00x. 'market' — that day's GeckoTerminal pool close, the ONLY source for a newly closed day since then, inside a gap-scaled drift band against the calendar-nearest known day. Today is priced provisionally from the running candle ('market-open', within x1.10 of the last known day) or carried forward. market_rates values for days through 2026-09-15 were banked by the first fetch after 00:00 UTC of the day they were first committed, so a value first committed on its own date is an open+~12min print rather than a close (alternating days; derive the list with git log -S); from 2026-09-16 a day is banked only after it closes. Closed days are never recomputed either way
- not included: no intraday rates and no pre-crawl days — a day with no payouts AND no market close gets no entry and is carried forward/back by classify.pin_rate
