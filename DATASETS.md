**`transfers`** — timestamp, transaction_hash, log_index, block_number, from.hash, to.hash, token.address_hash, total.value, total.decimals

- provenance: Blockscout v2 token-transfers (filter=from) → eth_getLogs fallback → 24h cross-check
- not included: outbound only; inbound lives in transfers_in/ (partial: tracked tokens only)

**`transfers_in`** — timestamp, transaction_hash, log_index, block_number, from.hash, to.hash, token.address_hash, total.value, total.decimals

- provenance: Blockscout v2 token-transfers (filter=to) → eth_getLogs fallback → 24h cross-check
- not included: every token the wallet ever received is stored, but only MOCA and MENTE are priced and counted — airdrops/spam are kept raw and excluded downstream

**`cognition_in`** — ts, val (MENTE token amount, float), from, tx, log_index, transaction_hash

- provenance: Blockscout v2 token-transfers into the Cognition Credits collector → eth_getLogs fallback → 24h cross-check
- not included: MENTE only, collector-inbound only; rows are pre-slimmed and val is a float token amount, not wei — see rows.py for the precision caveat

## oracle

| Dataset | Path | Rows | Size | Coverage | Cadence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `day_rates` | `day_rates.json` | 131 | 13.4 KB | 2026-04-24 → 2026-08-29 | one immutable entry per token per day |

**`day_rates`** — day_rates[symbol][YYYY-MM-DD] -> USD rate; day_rate_src[symbol][YYYY-MM-DD] -> 'implied'|'market'; plus last_accepted_rate, recon, pending_rate, open_day_rate, market_rates

- provenance: two sources, marked per day in day_rate_src: 'implied' — the day-implied payout rate from that day's $0.10 invoke cluster, anchored by the live Blockscout exchange rate; 'market' — that day's GeckoTerminal pool close, used only for a CLOSED day the implied oracle could not price (no >=5-row cluster) and only inside the same 5x sanity band as the live rate. Closed days are never recomputed either way
- not included: no intraday rates and no pre-crawl days — a day with no payouts AND no market close gets no entry and is carried forward/back by classify.pin_rate
